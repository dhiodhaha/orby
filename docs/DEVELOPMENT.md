# [App] desktop (Rust)

The spec and design live in WSL: `\\wsl.localhost\Ubuntu\home\dhio\clippy` (`docs/ORB_SPEC.md`, `DESIGN.md`).
Build on this Windows side.

## Orb prototype, milestone 1 (`apps/orb-proto`)
```
cargo build --release
target\release\orb-proto.exe
```
- **Orb:** the 48 px mercury drop, DirectComposition + Direct3D 11. The liquid-metal shader (`src/metal.hlsl`) is ported from Paper Shaders (Apache-2.0, see `NOTICE`).
- **Moving it:** drag it anywhere. On release it snaps to the nearest left or right edge. After 3 s it tucks half off the edge at 40%, and it slides out when the pointer comes within 20 px.
- **Highlight:** it leans toward the pointer, and follows the sun through the day (it dims after Isya).
- **Controls:** `Ctrl+Alt+Space` shows or hides it. Clicking it, or the tray menu, plays the 5 s prayer moment. Quit from the tray icon.
- **Log:** RAM and fps are written to `target\release\orb-proto.log`.

## Code layout (`apps/orb-proto/src`, after the refactor unit)
`main.rs` was split into modules with no change in behaviour. `App` in `main.rs` is still the single owner of all state; the modules are its children, so they read and write its fields directly.
| File | What lives there |
|---|---|
| `main.rs` | `App`, `with_app`, the shared ids (window messages, timers, hotkeys), the orb window's message dispatch, the animation tick and the loop |
| `orb.rs` | the drop: shader, `Spring`, docking, tuck, drag, proximity lean, show and hide |
| `pill.rs` | the notification pill window: show, dismiss, actions, hover pause |
| `dial.rs` | the half dial window: open, close, aim, keys, picks, `Ctrl+Alt+Space` |
| `prayer.rs` | prayer times, reminders (one exact timer), the Prayer panel window (`panel.rs` draws it) |
| `habits.rs` | focus and drink on `app-habits` (see the focus-drink section below) |
| `hook.rs` | the low-level mouse hook and its statics |
| `tray.rs` | the tray icon, its menu, and `WM_COMMAND` ids |
| `disk.rs`, `disk_model.rs`, `disk_panel.rs`, `disk_window.rs`, `disk_restore.rs` | the Disk unit: state, threads, pills and input; sizes, words, the moved-items ledger and the treemap; drawing and hits; its own window; Undo out of the Recycle Bin |
| `history.rs`, `history_model.rs`, `history_panel.rs` | the History unit: state and glue; days, levels and words; drawing and hits |
| `notices.rs`, `notices_model.rs`, `notices_panel.rs` | the notices unit: the queue every pill goes through, quiet times, full screen, mutes; the pure queue; the Notices panel. `pill.rs` now also draws the stack (peeks, spread, "N more") and the attention shake |
| `settings.rs`, `settings_model.rs`, `settings_panel.rs` | the settings unit: the Settings window and the first run, `settings.json`, the shortcut, the Run entry, Windows location; the values, cities, the shortcut capture and the steps; drawing and hits |
| `tasks.rs`, `tasks_model.rs`, `tasks_panel.rs`, `textfield.rs` | the Tasks unit: state and input, what the panel shows and does, drawing, the quick add field (added after the refactor) |
| `gfx.rs`, `ui.rs`, `panel.rs`, `metal.hlsl` | unchanged |

A new unit adds its timer or hotkey id in `main.rs` (one list, so ids on the orb window never collide), its menu id in `tray.rs`, and its own module.

## Focus and drink (focus-drink unit)
Focus runs on `app_habits::FocusTimer`, drink on `app_habits::Drink`, and notices during focus go through `app_habits::HeldNotices`. The ad hoc 25 min timer is gone.
- **Opening:** dial, then Focus or Drink (a click on the item, or Enter with its branch open and the parent aimed), or tray, then "Open the Focus panel" / "Open the Drink panel".
- **Dial branches:** Focus fans out 15 / 25 / 50 min, Drink 150 / 250 / 500 ml.
  - Mouse: 120 ms of steady aim on the parent opens the branch. Past the main band the pointer aims a child by direction; a click picks it.
  - Keys: 4 or 5 (or Enter, or the arrow toward the screen centre) opens the branch on its default child (25, 250). Up and down move in it, Enter picks, the arrow toward the edge steps back to the parent (Enter there opens the panel), Esc closes the branch, then the dial.
  - Keys and mouse: after any dial key the keys stay in charge until the pointer moves 32 px from where it was at that key, so a nudge of the mouse never changes a keyboard pick. A deliberate move takes over as before.
  - While a session runs, the Focus branch is Pause (or Resume), Skip to break and Stop. The Focus chip carries the focus ring.
  - A child pick confirms with a pill and Undo ("+250 ml 1250 / 2000 ml", "Focus 25 min").
- **Focus panel:**
  - Setup: the knob (5 to 90 min, 5 min detents; drag it, scroll, or up and down), presets 15 / 25 / 50 (Custom lights up in between), the task row, breaks (Change steps 5/15, 10/20, 15/30), "Start the next session by itself", and Start.
  - Running: the 168 px ring (dotted is time left, solid is spent), the countdown, session dots, the held-notices line, and Pause or Resume, Skip to break, Stop. On a break: Start focus and Stop.
- **Drink panel:** the glass (dotted empty part, moonlight water), the total and pace, +150 / +250 ml / +500, today's log (latest first, Undo on the latest only), the last 7 days with the dashed goal line, and the next reminder.
- **Prayer pauses focus:** "Memasuki waktu" calls `pause_for_prayer`; after the prayer pill, "Focus paused for Isya · 24:12 left" offers Resume (pill, panel or dial). Prayer is never counted or logged.
- **Held notices:** during focus (and a pause you made), normal notices wait: the drink notice (since 2026-11-07 no longer the −30 / −10 min prayer reminders, which show at once). At the break one pill arrives, "Break 5 min · 2 waited" with Show (since the notices unit, Show opens the Notices panel, where they wait). Stopping early gives "Notices · 1 waited". Urgent "Memasuki waktu" always passes.
  - A prayer pause holds nothing (`FocusTimer::is_holding` is false, ORB_SPEC holds notices "during focus" only): what waited so far comes as "Notices · 1 waited" after "Focus paused", and new notices show at once until you resume.
  - A held "10 menit lagi Subuh" goes stale at Subuh: "Memasuki waktu Subuh" drops it, and the break and Show drop any whose prayer time has passed, so it is never replayed late.
- **Drink reminders:** one exact timer to `Drink::next_reminder`. A reminder due during focus waits for the break and rides on the break pill ("Break 5 min · minum dulu?" with +250 ml).
  - Launch: `Drink::start_watching(now)` starts the reminder clock at launch (time before it counts as away), so a launch never reminds at once, and a reminder never moves past 21:00 (an overdue one moving to now, or one waiting for a late break, is dropped).
  - Away: `GetLastInputInfo` past 5 idle minutes pauses the clock. Then raw input is watched (only while away) and the first key or mouse move reschedules.
  - Lock: `WTSRegisterSessionNotification` makes a lock reset the clock and an unlock restart it.
  - After 21:00 the timer sleeps until 08:01 tomorrow (the log says "no drink reminder before Sat 08:01").
- **Timers:** the focus timer wakes once per degree of the ring (every 4.2 s for 25 min, at least 1 s) and exactly at the phase end. The panels tick once a second only while a countdown is on screen.
- **Data:** `%APPDATA%\orb-proto\habits.json` holds the drink log, the finished focus sessions and the focus setup. Times are Unix seconds (UTC), converted at the file's edge with the zone's rule for each date (`store::unix`, `store::local`); `"format": 1` marks this, and an older file without it (local wall-clock seconds) is converted on load. It is rewritten whole after each change (write, then rename). A corrupt file is kept aside as `habits.json.corrupt`. `ORB_PROTO_DATA` points the app at another folder, so test drives never touch the real log.
- **Driving it:** `.audit\fd-lib.ps1` holds the helpers (`Start-Orb <dataDir>`, `Cmd`, `Hk`, `Click <class> x y`, `Shot`). Shots are in `.audit\shots\focus-drink-*.png`. `.audit\refactor-drive.ps1` predates the branches: its key 4 now opens the Focus branch instead of starting focus.
- **Checked (2026-11-06, white theme, 96 dpi):** every flow above in the real exe, a 1 min session through to its break, the log surviving a restart, and idle CPU 46.9 ms over 30 s (working set 45.7 MB). Fix round (23:00): a launch at 23:01 on fresh and on real data schedules nothing, an old local-time file migrates (22:39 stays 22:39 on screen, 7 h less on disk), a held "10 menit lagi Subuh" is dropped at "Memasuki waktu Subuh", a held drink notice comes back after "Focus paused" and the next one shows at once, a 15 px mouse nudge keeps the keyboard aim, and idle CPU is 46.9 ms over 30 s (45.6 MB).
- **Not done:** a real task picker (the task row shows "No task"; tasks come with the Tasks panel), the "This week" focus strip (History is in, see its section), Drink settings (goal knob, glass sizes, puasa mode, after-prayer), snooze `S`, the custom bottle button, the badge for a drink reminder while the orb is hidden (it is skipped and the clock restarts), sounds, the dark theme on screen (the dev PC is in white), mark mode (press and flick) on the dial, and resuming focus when the prayer pill is closed (ORB_SPEC 6.1 says so, but DESIGN.md v6.2 shows an explicit Resume, and closing the pill before going to pray must not restart the clock; a prayer pause now holds nothing, so leaving it paused costs no notices).

- **Opening:** dial, then Tasks (click, Enter, or key 1), or tray, then "Open the Tasks panel". Esc clears the field first, then closes; a click outside closes.
- **First run:** one private space "Personal" with an Inbox list, nothing else. `tasks.json` next to the database keeps this install's device id and the inbox id. Sample tasks come only from the tray test item, only into a store with no tasks, and each says "Sample task" (their folder is "Home (sample)").
- **Panel (780 × 560):**
  - Sidebar: Inbox, Today, Upcoming with open counts, then Spaces: a lock chip and "private" (a shared space would get the people chip), folders nested 20 px per level with a chevron and a count, lists with a count. The chevron folds a folder; clicking a folder opens its smart view (every list inside it). "+" names a new list in the open space; `Home/Groceries` makes the folder too. The sidebar foot says "On this PC only · sync is off".
  - Header: breadcrumb, list name (20/500), and Share, which only says that sharing comes with sync.
  - Quick add: a 40 px field with a focus halo. Parsed parts become chips as you type (date, time, repeat, `#list`, `/folder`, `@person`), and the line under it restates what it understood ("Tomorrow 09:00, in Groceries, for you.") with the grammar. Enter or Add saves. Without `#list` a task goes to the open list (in a folder view, its first list), otherwise the Inbox; in Today a task with no date is due today. `@` knows the Windows user name.
  - Tabs To do / Done with counts. Rows: the 18 px checkbox, title, a second muted line (the list name in Today, Upcoming and folders, the first line of the notes, "Added by" for someone else's task), the due ("Today 17:00", "Tomorrow 09:00", "Overdue · Wed" in ink 500, never red, "No date"), and the assignee avatar (an initial, "Y" for You as in the frame; a dashed circle when unassigned). A repeating task has the repeat icon and names its next date: "Tomorrow 09:00 · every Sat", "14 Nov 09:00 · every Sat", or just "Every Wed 09:00" when the weekday within the coming week already says which day. Long titles end in an ellipsis. Hover shows a delete button. Done rows say "Done 23:41"; ticking one reopens it. Ticking a repeating task moves it to its next date instead of closing it, and the label shows the new date.
  - Today: overdue and due today, with the prayer times still ahead as dashed markers among the timed tasks (never rows, never checkable).
  - Footer: "2 done today · last: You finished Beras 5 kg at 23:41"; it opens Done.
  - The wheel scrolls what it is over: the rows, or the sidebar's tree of spaces when there are more lists than fit (a thin thumb shows it can scroll; Inbox, Today and Upcoming stay put). Opening a list, from the sidebar, a new list or the dial, scrolls the tree just enough to show it.
- **Text field:** a minimal Direct2D field (`textfield.rs` edits, `tasks_panel.rs` draws). Typing, Backspace, Delete, arrows, Home, End, Ctrl for words, Shift to select, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+V, and a click places the caret. The caret does not blink, so an open panel draws nothing until you type. The panel window is the only one that takes the keyboard: it drops `WS_EX_NOACTIVATE` while Tasks is open and hands the foreground back on close.
- **Due pill:** one exact timer (`TIMER_TASKS`) to whichever comes first: the next timed task's minute, the minute after the next prayer marker in Today passes (so it leaves an open Today on time), or midnight. At midnight it loads the new day's prayer times before it redraws, so an open Today shows the new date with its markers. At the due minute: "Beras 5 kg due 23:39" with a checkbox chip and Done (two at once: "2 tasks due" with Open). It goes through the held-notices gate like any normal notice. While the orb is hidden it is skipped and only the badge counts it.
- **Badge:** open tasks past due (an all-day task from before today, or a timed one whose minute has come) are a count on the drop's upper shoulder, on the side away from the edge so it stays readable while tucked. It is drawn with Direct2D into the drop's own swap chain after the shader, so the drop stays one plain mercury drop. The dial's Tasks value is live ("1 overdue", or "2 today"), and so is Today's ("3 left": what is open in the Today view; it replaced the old hard-coded "4 left").
- **Tray ids:** 12 open the Tasks panel, 13 test: add sample tasks (empty store only), 14 add calendar link from the clipboard, 15 open the Today panel, 16 test: event pill 10 min before, 17 test: event pill starting now, 18 read the calendar now, 19 remove the calendar link.
- **Driving it:** `.audit\tasks-lib.ps1` adds `Type-Text` (WM_CHAR to the panel), `Key` (WM_KEYDOWN), `Wheel <delta> <x> <y>` (x under 228 scrolls the sidebar), `TShot` and `OrbShot` (the drop at 4×) to `fd-lib.ps1`. `.audit\tasks-drive.ps1` runs the whole flow on a throwaway folder (`ORB_PROTO_DATA`) in about 3 minutes and saves `.audit\shots\tasks-drive-*.png`.

## Tasks window (tasks "both")
The small Tasks panel stays as it is (quick add and the lists); every task also has its own window. Code: `tasks_window.rs` (the window, its state and input), `tasks_window_panel.rs` (drawing and hits; 2 tests), and the window's model in `tasks_model.rs` (`groups`, `window_items`, `reorder`, `edited_title`, `picked_due`, `restored`; 6 tests).
- **Window:** class `OrbTasks`, built like the Disk window: `WS_POPUP` with `WS_EX_APPWINDOW` (taskbar, Alt+Tab), not topmost, takes the keyboard on open, a click elsewhere leaves it open. 600 × 680 logical px, or the work area minus 32 px (at least 420), centred on the orb's screen each time it opens; opening it while open brings it to the front. A 44 px title band ("Tasks", the open count as a rolling number, the close button; its empty part drags). The window and its controls carry the `depth::lip`. Like Disk and Settings it draws no outer shadow (the window region clips it, and `depth::follow`'s shadow window is topmost). The frame timer runs only while a ripple or rolling number moves; the swap chain is dropped while hidden. Esc (after the menu, an edit and the field's text), the close button, Alt+F4 or the taskbar close it.
- **Resize and maximize:** like the Disk window (see "Resize and maximize" under Disk): edges resize down to 600 × 420, a maximize / restore button left of close, double click on the band, Win+Up and Snap; the field, rows and Undo bar widen up to a 720 px column; a wider (maximized) window centres that column so a due date stays near its title.
- **Opening:** the panel's "All tasks" button (left of Share), tray "Open all tasks" (id 80), or dial, More, then "All tasks" (the sixth child, after Settings; it reads "12 open").
- **Same data:** it reads and writes the panel's `app.db` store and every change goes through `tasks::commit`, which redraws the panel, the badge, the due timer and this window. The due timer's minute and midnight ticks redraw it too.
- **List:** Today (overdue and due today), Upcoming, No date, then Done (latest first, folded by default; its heading unfolds it). Open tasks sort by due, then by hand order, as the panel's Today does. Empty groups are left out; with no tasks: "No tasks yet". Rows ripple in on open; group counts roll.
- **Add:** the field at the top uses quick add's grammar (`besok 9am`, `#list`); the line under it restates what it understood. Without `#list` a task goes to the Inbox. Enter or Add saves.
- **Rows:** the checkbox (a repeating task moves to its next date, a done one reopens), the title with its list name muted (not for the Inbox), the due ("Overdue · Thu" in ink 500). Hover or the keyboard ring shows Due (the calendar button) and Delete.
- **Edit:** double-click the title, or Enter or F2 on a focused task. Enter saves (trimmed; an empty or unchanged title saves nothing), Esc puts the old title back, a click elsewhere saves.
- **Due:** the menu offers Today, Tomorrow, Next week and No date (D opens it from the keyboard). A new date keeps the task's time of day.
- **Delete:** the row's Delete button or the Delete key. An Undo bar shows for 8 s ("Deleted Beras 5 kg", Undo, or Ctrl+Z). The store keeps deletes as tombstones, so Undo makes the task again in its list with the same title, notes, due, repeat, assignee and order, under a new id.

On macOS `mac/cg.rs` draws with Core Graphics into a premultiplied sRGB bitmap at the screen's backing scale, y down, and `mac/text.rs` sets the bundled Inter through Core Text with the same `wght`/`opsz` axes and `tnum`/`cv11` features.
- **The orb:** `mac/metal.msl` is `metal.hlsl` line for line in the Metal Shading Language, compiled at run time with `newLibraryWithSource` (the command line tools have no `metal` compiler). Same uniforms and resting values (time 1.7, distortion 0.1), the sun angle from today's prayer times and dimmed after Isya, still at rest; rendered at 2x into a `CAMetalLayer` and filtered down. Keep the two shader files in step.
- **Build and run (on the Mac):**
  ```
  source ~/.cargo/env
  cargo build -p orb-proto                 # debug
  scripts/mac-bundle.sh [release]          # builds, quits a running Orby, installs ~/Applications/Orby.app (LSUIElement, ad-hoc signed)
  open ~/Applications/Orby.app
  ```
  Checks: `cargo clippy -p orb-proto --all-targets -- -D warnings`, `cargo test -p orb-proto` (41 tests; Windows-only tests are cfg'd out with their modules).
- **Snapshot mode (both systems):** `ORB_SNAPSHOT=<dir>` (or on macOS `orb-proto --snapshot <dir>`) renders fixed scenes (Yogyakarta, Kemenag, 10 Nov 2026 14:37:25) to PNG at 2x and exits: `dial`, `dial-branch`, `pill-drink`, `pill-prayer`, `prayer`, each `-light` and `-dark`; on macOS also `orb-day` and `orb-night` from Metal. The PNG writer (`png.rs`) is uncompressed and deterministic, so two runs with the same pixels give the same bytes: on Windows the snapshots before and after the Canvas change were byte-identical (MD5 of all ten files).
- **What works on the Mac:** the 48 px drop always on top (a borderless non-activating `NSPanel`, level above floating, on every Space and over full-screen apps, no Dock icon: activation policy Accessory); drag it anywhere, it snaps to the nearest left or right edge of that screen's `visibleFrame` (menu bar and Dock respected, any screen); after 3 s it tucks half off at 40%, and slides out when the pointer comes within 20 px (global mouse-moved monitor: no permission needed, nothing runs while the mouse is still). The menu bar item (a template drop icon) has Show/Hide Orby, Prayer and Quit. Light and dark follow the system appearance when a window opens.

## macOS phase 2a: Disk, the More branch, start at login (branch `orby-mac-2`)
- **Shared Disk:** `disk.rs` (state machine), `disk_model.rs`, `disk_panel.rs` and `disk_scenes.rs` build on both systems. `disk_panel.rs` draws through the `Canvas` (tracked big numbers, wrapped heights, baselines, the clipped and middle-cut text are `Canvas`/`canvas.rs` calls now); on Windows `D2dCanvas` replays the same Direct2D calls, so the ten Disk scenes in snapshot mode, light and dark (20 PNGs, plus the 10 from phase 1), are byte-identical before and after (MD5 of all 30). `frame_cost_of_a_big_list` is unchanged (2.5 ms at 756 x 704, 3.6 ms at 2560 x 1392, release). Everything the state machine asks of the system goes through `disk_sys.rs`: on Windows it wraps the old Win32 code (timers, `WM_APP_DISK`, the shell, the folder picker, `disk_restore`), on macOS `mac/disk.rs`. Keys are Windows virtual-key numbers on both (`disk::VK_*`); the Mac maps its key codes. Words that name the bin and the file viewer live in `disk_model::os` (Recycle Bin / Trash, Open in Explorer / Show in Finder).
- **The window (`mac/disk_win.rs`):** a titled, resizable `NSWindow` with a transparent, full-size titlebar and the traffic lights hidden: it draws its own 44 px band like Windows (Disk, zoom, close). The band's empty part drags (`performWindowDragWithEvent`), a double click or the zoom button zooms (square corners while zoomed), the edges resize down to 756 x 480. It activates Orby and takes the keyboard when it opens (Orby stays an accessory app: no Dock icon or Cmd+Tab entry). Keys: arrows, Return, Space, Tab, Delete (up a level), Cmd+Delete (the confirm sheet, as in the Finder), forward Delete, Esc (the sheet first, then the window), Cmd+W, Cmd+Q (quits Orby). Trackpad scrolling adds up pixel deltas into rows (36 px a row), a wheel notch is a row. Hover, the treemap label card and the rows' ripple work as on Windows. Input marks the window dirty and the run loop draws once per turn (60 Hz only while something moves); the bitmap is dropped when the window closes and the scan's tree 3 min later.
- **Mac specifics:** the start screen offers the home folder (the default for Scan again and the low-space pill's Find big folders), Macintosh HD and Scan a folder (`NSOpenPanel`, run after the click with the app not borrowed). Move to Trash only (`NSFileManager trashItemAtURL`, through app-disk); Undo is `app_disk::put_back`, a rename out of the Trash that never overwrites or makes folders. Show in Finder selects the item (`activateFileViewerSelectingURLs`); the Trash row opens `~/.Trash` in the Finder (never empties it); System files opens Storage settings. Workers wake the main thread with `performSelectorOnMainThread:` on the menu target (retried 20 ms later if the app was busy, so no report is lost).
- **Permissions:** without Full Disk Access a macOS app that opens Desktop, Documents, Downloads, iCloud Drive, `~/Library/CloudStorage`, other apps' containers or the Photos, Photo Booth, Music and TV libraries is stopped by a consent prompt until someone answers. `app_disk::protected_folders()` lists them when `full_disk_access()` is false (it opens `~/Library/Application Support/com.apple.TCC/TCC.db`, which never prompts), the app's scans and Safe to clean leave them out (`SkipReason::Protected`), and the card "Some folders need Full Disk Access" opens System Settings, Privacy & Security, Full Disk Access. The walk's watchdog also leaves out any other folder the system holds for 4 s, so a scan always ends. Orby never grants anything itself.
- **APFS:** duplicate copies carry what moving them frees (`ATTR_CMNEXT_PRIVATESIZE`), so a clone of the kept copy counts close to nothing and a group that shares blocks says "shares space (APFS clone)"; reclaim and the selection count allocated bytes.
- **Dial and menu bar:** More fans out Downloads, Disk, History, Notices, Settings and All tasks (a steady aim, a click or key 6 opens it; Up/Down, Return, Esc); Disk opens the window, the others say Coming to Mac. The menu bar item adds Disk… and Start at login (`SMAppService.mainAppService`, off by default, a checkmark shows its state).
- **Low space:** every 10 min (and at launch) the startup disk is read; under 10% free the pill "Macintosh HD has 13 GB left" with Find big folders (scans the home folder and opens Disk); then only after 5 more points, kept in `disk.json`, once a day in the queue.
- **Test commands** (`~/orby-t.sh <cmd>` on the Mac wraps the osascript line above): `disk`, `disk-close`, `disk-state` (one log line: screen, scan, duplicates, selection, sheet, cards), `disk-scan:<path>`, `disk-tab:files|types|folders`, `disk-row:<i>`, `disk-file:<i>`, `disk-dupes`, `disk-dupes-check`, `disk-dupe-pick:<g>,<r>`, `disk-clean`, `disk-back`, `disk-confirm`, `disk-cancel`, `disk-move` (refused unless every item is under the temporary folder), `disk-undo`, `disk-key:<vk>`, `disk-wheel:<x>,<y>,<delta>`, `disk-shot:<png>` (the window's view drawn to a PNG, no screen recording needed), `disk-bench:<frames>`, `disk-resources`, `more`, `more-pick`, `dial-shot:<png>`, `login`.

### Shared units over `sys` (glue)

`notices.rs`, `habits.rs` (focus and drink), `history.rs`, `store.rs` and `dial_menu.rs` build on both systems, like `disk.rs` over `disk_sys.rs`:
- **`sys.rs`** is what they ask of the system: named timers (`Timer::{Tasks, Focus, Drink, CalCue, CalRead, Notices, Panel}`, `arm(app, t, ms)` / `disarm`, Win32 SetTimer's meaning: repeating until disarmed), `now()` / `ms_into_second()` / `tick_ms()` / `unix_ms()`, `unix_of_local` / `local_of_unix`, `idle_ms()`, `watch_input`, `shift_down` / `ctrl_down`, `read_clipboard`, `open_url`, `play_notify_sound`, `reduced_motion`, `log`, `start_anim`, `data_dir()`, full screen and presenting (`watch_foreground`, `foreground_seen`, `full_screen`, `presenting`), and `vk::VK_*` (Windows virtual-key numbers, the keys every unit reads). Windows: `sys::imp` over Win32 and the existing windows; macOS: `mac/sys.rs` (timers are deadlines in `App::timers`, fired by `tick`, waited for by `schedule`; the clipboard and sound through `mac/input.rs`).
- **The window modules keep their paths on macOS:** `mac/shim.rs` provides `crate::pill`, `crate::orb`, `crate::dial`, `crate::prayer`, `crate::settings` (only what the shared units call), `crate::log_line` and `crate::start_anim`; `mac/panels.rs` is `crate::panels` (`Kind`, `Hit`, `open_panel`, `close_panel`, `refresh`, `redraw`, `take_keyboard`, `release_keyboard`, `drill_in`, `back_or_close`, `wants_arrows`, `wants_enter`, `on_arrow`, `on_esc`). Drawing entry points take a `&mut dyn Canvas` (`habits::draw_focus_panel`, `draw_drink_panel`, `history::draw_panel`, `notices::draw_panel`); Windows' panels.rs passes `D2dCanvas::new(&app.g, dc)`, so the 264 snapshot PNGs are byte-identical.
- **`dial_menu.rs`** decides what the dial's ring and branches show and what a pick does (Focus 15/25/50 or Pause/Skip/Stop, Drink's glasses, More's six children) for Windows' dial.rs and the Mac dial. Units not shared yet sit behind its `pending` module (on the Mac a plain child and "Coming to Mac").
- **On the Mac** the queue is `app.notices` (the old `App::post` queue is gone), with `app.habits` and `app.history`; Focus, Drink, History and Notices open in the panel window (sized per panel, redrawn only when something changed or while a number rolls). Stubbed: full screen and presenting are never seen, the drop has no focus ring or badge yet, the pill has no shake or stack, a lock is not told apart from idle (idle covers it), and Tasks, Today, Downloads, Settings, All tasks say Coming to Mac.
